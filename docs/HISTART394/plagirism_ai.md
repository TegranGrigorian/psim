# Plagiarism

We live in an age of sampling, quotation, and communal authorship. Despite this, plagiarism is treated seriously at the University of Michigan. It means taking credit for someone else’s words, work, interpretations, or unique ideas without giving proper credit to the original creator—even if the site you use has no specific author (e.g., Wikipedia). Any instance of plagiarism in this course may result in a failing grade.

For more information, see:

# Artificial Intelligence

Assignments and final papers should use AI only for basic inquiries—**not for writing**—and declare the AI model, prompts, and results in the bibliography.
