## Overview
This project is built for HISTART 394, a University of Michigan course on how factors of power employ tactics to deconstruct and mobilize space to align towards their intrests. Because this project is for a course at the University of Michigan(UMich), it follows the University's honor code and HISTART 394 AI/Academic policies. The use of AI is discloused in this document.

## AI Usage
### Permitted AI Usage

#### Document Construction
AI has been used in the construction of documents regarding this project **BESIDES** the abstract, including the AI policy document itself (hi copilot). As such, all AI usage is disclosed here.

### Data Analysis
AI has been used to capture data on the city of Ann Arbor and any other relevent locations. Having a human do this would monotonous and time-consuming, whereas AI can efficiently gather and organize large amounts of data. However, in the documents constructed later on, a large part of the data is either scrapped or harvested via open-source platforms, public records and other publicly available sources. As such, those sources will not be using AI. The reason I am pointing this out here is in thhe case that any questions arise regarding the origin of the data, it is clear which parts were AI-assisted and which parts were not.

### Code Assistance
I am not the GOAT of software design, as such I will employ AI to assist in coding, debugging, and optimizing software components related to this project. However, I will make myself clear, AI **WILL** be a tool to assist me in the simulation portion of this assingment; not to replace my own understanding and effort in the development process. I will code nearly everything myself, using AI only as a supplementary aid when necessary. 

Lastly, some specified portion of this assingment, specifically the website development, will use an agentic AI where I will not have full control over the AI's actions, but I will still oversee and guide its contributions to ensure alignment with project goals. I chose this because the website development will benefti with AI intervention and more importantly it is not important for the core objectives of the assingment and I have limited time to complete this.

### Prohibited AI Usage

#### Academic Assignments
AI is not used to generate original content for the abstract, report, or any other written assignments that are required to be completed by the student according to HISTART 394 course policies. This ensures that the work submitted for academic evaluation remains the student's own intellectual contribution and adheres to the University's honor code.

## Conclusion

In summary, AI is employed in this project for document construction, data analysis, and code assistance, with clear boundaries and oversight to ensure academic integrity and alignment with project goals. All AI usage is disclosed, and prohibited uses are strictly avoided to maintain the student's own intellectual contribution and adherence to the University's honor code.
